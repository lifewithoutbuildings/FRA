### Q2. Adjusting entries and final accounts (8 marks)

**Bright Tutorials Ltd** was set up on 1 January. Its **unadjusted trial balance at 31 January** is:

| Account | Debit (₹) | Credit (₹) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Equipment | 24,000 | |
| Supplies | 1,600 | |
| Prepaid insurance | 1,200 | |
| Cash | 3,400 | |
| Dividends | 500 | |
| Salaries expense | 2,000 | |
| Rent expense | 700 | |
| Trade payables | | 900 |
| Unearned tuition | | 2,400 |
| Share capital | | 20,000 |
| Tuition revenue | | 10,100 |
| **Total** | **33,400** | **33,400** |

Additional information at 31 January:
- (a) Equipment has a 5-year life and no salvage value.
- (b) Supplies on hand are ₹600.
- (c) The ₹1,200 insurance premium (paid 1 January) is for one year.
- (d) Of the unearned tuition, ₹900 has now been earned.
- (e) Salaries of ₹600 for the last few days are unpaid.
- (f) Tuition of ₹500 was provided to students but not yet billed.

**Required:** (i) the adjusting journal entries; (ii) the statement of profit and loss for January; (iii) the balance sheet at 31 January.

### Q4. Effect of a mis-booking (1 mark)

A firm received **₹50,000 cash in advance** from a customer and recorded the **entire amount as revenue**, although no service has yet been provided. The effect on net income for the period is:

## Answer key

> [!success]- Q1 — debit/credit balances (4)
> a. **Credit** — accumulated depreciation is a *contra-asset*; it carries a credit balance. b. **Debit** — prepaid insurance is an asset. c. **Credit** — interest payable is a liability. d. **Debit** — drawings reduce equity and carry a debit balance. → [[10-Journal-Ledger-and-Trial-Balance#1. The rule that governs everything]]

> [!success]- Q2 — Bright Tutorials, full solution (8)
> **(i) Adjusting entries**
>
> | Adj | Entry | Dr (₹) | Cr (₹) |
> |---|---|---:|---:|
> | (a) | Depreciation expense · To Accumulated depreciation (24,000 ÷ 5 ÷ 12) | 400 | 400 |
> | (b) | Supplies expense · To Supplies (1,600 − 600) | 1,000 | 1,000 |
> | (c) | Insurance expense · To Prepaid insurance (1,200 ÷ 12) | 100 | 100 |
> | (d) | Unearned tuition · To Tuition revenue | 900 | 900 |
> | (e) | Salaries expense · To Salaries payable | 600 | 600 |
> | (f) | Trade receivables · To Tuition revenue | 500 | 500 |
>
> **(ii) Statement of profit and loss — January**
>
> | | ₹ |
> |---|---:|
> | Tuition revenue (10,100 + 900 + 500) | **11,500** |
> | Salaries (2,000 + 600) | (2,600) |
> | Supplies expense | (1,000) |
> | Rent | (700) |
> | Depreciation | (400) |
> | Insurance | (100) |
> | **Net profit** | **6,700** |
>
> *(Dividends ₹500 are **not** an expense — deducted from retained earnings: 6,700 − 500 = **6,200**.)*
>
> **(iii) Balance sheet — 31 January**
>
> | Assets | ₹ | Liabilities & Equity | ₹ |
> |---|---:|---|---:|
> | Equipment (24,000 − 400) | 23,600 | Trade payables | 900 |
> | Supplies | 600 | Unearned tuition (2,400 − 900) | 1,500 |
> | Prepaid insurance | 1,100 | Salaries payable | 600 |
> | Trade receivables | 500 | Share capital | 20,000 |
> | Cash | 3,400 | Retained earnings | 6,200 |
> | **Total** | **29,200** | **Total** | **29,200** |
>
> → [[11-Adjusting-Entries-and-Final-Accounts#1. Why adjusting entries exist]]

> [!success]- Q3 — omitted depreciation (1)
> **b. Assets overstated, equity overstated.** No accumulated depreciation recorded → **assets overstated**; no depreciation expense → profit and therefore **equity overstated**. → [[11-Adjusting-Entries-and-Final-Accounts#6. Depreciation via accumulated depreciation (Day 8)]]

> [!success]- Q4 — advance recorded as revenue (1)
> **a. Overstated by ₹50,000.** The ₹50,000 should sit in **unearned revenue** (a liability) until earned; booking it as revenue overstates income by the full amount. → [[11-Adjusting-Entries-and-Final-Accounts#1. Why adjusting entries exist]]

> [!success]- Q5 — prepaid rent (1)
> **b. Prepaid expense (an asset).** Cash paid before the benefit is consumed is an asset until it expires. → [[11-Adjusting-Entries-and-Final-Accounts#1. Why adjusting entries exist]]

> [!success]- Q6 — the table (4)
> **Year 1 (2023):** Retained earnings = revenue − expenses = (600 + 200) − 300 = **₹500 mn**. Liabilities = Assets − CC − RE = 2,000 − 400 − 500 = **₹1,100 mn**.
> **Year 2 (2024):** Profit = (1,800 + 700) − 1,900 = 600. Retained earnings = 500 + 600 − **50 dividends** = **₹1,050 mn**. Assets = L + CC + RE = 1,200 + 400 + 1,050 = **₹2,650 mn**.
>
> | | 2023 | 2024 |
> |---|---:|---:|
> | Assets | 2,000 | **2,650** |
> | Liabilities | **1,100** | 1,200 |
> | Retained earnings | **500** | **1,050** |
>
> → [[08-The-Accounting-Equation#2. The expanded equation — bringing in revenue and expenses]] · [[11-Adjusting-Entries-and-Final-Accounts#9. Statement of changes in equity]]

> [!success]- Q7 — zero opening balance (1)
> **c. Sales revenue.** Revenue is a temporary account, closed to zero at year-end and **restarting at ₹0**. Cash, accumulated depreciation and share capital are balance-sheet accounts that carry forward. → [[11-Adjusting-Entries-and-Final-Accounts#5. Closing entries (Day 8)]]
