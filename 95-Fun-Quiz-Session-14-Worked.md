# 95 · Fun Quiz (Session 14) — Worked & Mapped

> [!info] Why this is the most important prep note for Quiz 3
> This is the **professor's own revision quiz**, run in the last long-term-assets session. It is the closest thing you have to a Quiz-3 blueprint: it samples **every** sub-topic (capitalisation, disposal, change in estimate, part-year, revaluation-on-disposal, exchange both ways, intangibles) at exactly the difficulty and phrasing the professor favours. **Attempt each cold, then reveal.** Same format as [[92-Past-Paper-Breakdown]] / [[93-Past-Papers-Review-Quiz-2-4]].

## Every question, worked

> [!question] Q1 — SLM first-year depreciation *(what capitalises)*
> Used tractor **$17,500**. Before use: new tyres **$1,100**, overhaul **$1,400**, first tank of fuel **$750**. Life 6 yrs, residual $2,000. First-year SLM depreciation?
>> [!success]- Reveal — **$3,000**
>> Capitalise tyres + overhaul (make it ready); **exclude fuel** (runs it). Cost = 17,500 + 1,100 + 1,400 = **20,000**.
>> Dep = (20,000 − 2,000)/6 = **$3,000**. *(Adding fuel → $3,125, the wrong option.)*

> [!question] Q2 — Disposal of equipment
> Cost **$64,800**, accumulated depreciation **$36,000**, disposed 1 April, sold **$12,000**. Gain or loss?
>> [!success]- Reveal — **$16,800 loss**
>> NBV = 64,800 − 36,000 = 28,800; cash 12,000 < NBV → **loss 16,800**.
>> `Dr Cash 12,000 / Dr Accum.Dep 36,000 / Dr Loss 16,800 → Cr Equipment 64,800 (gross)`. "1 April" is a distractor.

> [!question] Q3 — Revision of useful life
> Computer ₹90,000, residual ₹10,000, life 8 yrs. After 2 yrs, now lasts **3 more years**, residual ₹10,000. SLM. Revised depreciation?
>> [!success]- Reveal — **₹20,000/yr**
>> Original dep 10,000/yr → accumulated 20,000 → carrying **70,000**. Revised = (70,000 − 10,000)/3 = **₹20,000**. Prospective — no back-entry.

> [!question] Q4 — Part-year depreciation
> Machine bought **1 July**, cost ₹34,000, life 5 yrs, residual ₹2,000. Year ended **31 March**?
>> [!success]- Reveal — **₹4,800**
>> Annual = (34,000 − 2,000)/5 = 6,400. Jul→Mar = **9 months** → 6,400 × 9/12 = **₹4,800**.

> [!question] Q5 — Revaluation then immediate disposal
> Machine BV ₹3,00,000 revalued to ₹3,50,000 (reserve ₹50,000), sold immediately for ₹3,20,000. Amount transferred to **General Reserve**?
>> [!success]- Reveal — **₹20,000** (net)
>> The full ₹50,000 surplus transfers RR → General Reserve; the ₹30,000 loss on sale (3,20,000 − revalued 3,50,000) hits P&L separately; the **net** addition to reserves = 50,000 − 30,000 = **₹20,000** = proceeds − **original** BV (3,20,000 − 3,00,000). If the exam instead asks what leaves the *revaluation reserve*, that's the whole **₹50,000**. The reserve transfer never runs through P&L.

> [!question] Q6 — Exchange, **similar** assets (vehicle for vehicle)
> Old vehicle BV ₹2,00,000, FV ₹2,20,000; cash ₹1,70,000; invoice ₹4,00,000. New vehicle recorded at?
>> [!success]- Reveal — **₹3,70,000**
>> Similar assets → **no commercial substance** → **BV + cash** = 2,00,000 + 1,70,000 = **₹3,70,000**. Fair value and invoice price are ignored; **no gain/loss**.

> [!question] Q7 — Exchange, **dissimilar** assets (vehicle for machine)
> Same figures, but a **machine** is acquired. New machine recorded at? Journal entry?
>> [!success]- Reveal — **₹3,90,000**
>> Dissimilar → **commercial substance** → **FV + cash** = 2,20,000 + 1,70,000 = **₹3,90,000**. The ₹20,000 (FV − BV) is a **gain**.
>> ```
>> Dr Machine (new)            3,90,000
>> Dr Accum. Dep — Vehicle       (clears old asset)
>>     Cr Vehicle (gross, old)
>>     Cr Cash                   1,70,000
>>     Cr Gain on exchange          20,000
>> ```

> [!question] Q8 — Internally generated brand
> Bought a brand for **₹50 lakhs**, spent **₹10 lakhs** more to strengthen it. Carrying amount (ignore amortisation)?
>> [!success]- Reveal — **₹50 lakhs**
>> Purchased brand capitalised; the ₹10 lakhs to strengthen is **internally generated → expensed**. *(Adding it → ₹60 lakhs is the trap.)*

> [!question] Q9 — Software development costs
> Research **$2,50,000**, development **$1,75,000**, copyright 40 yrs, viability **5 yrs**. Balance-sheet value after 1 year?
>> [!success]- Reveal — **$1,40,000**
>> Research expensed; capitalise development **$1,75,000**; amortise over **useful life 5 yrs** (not 40) → 35,000. Carrying = 1,75,000 − 35,000 = **$1,40,000**.

## Answer key

| Q | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ans | $3,000 | $16,800 loss | ₹20,000/yr | ₹4,800 | ₹20,000 | ₹3,70,000 | ₹3,90,000 | ₹50 lakhs | $1,40,000 |

> [!tip] The five rules this quiz drills hardest
> 1. **Only "get-it-ready" costs capitalise** — fuel/running costs out (Q1); brand-building out (Q8); research out (Q9).
> 2. **Disposal credits the asset at gross**, not net (Q2).
> 3. **Estimate/method changes are prospective** — carrying amount over remaining life (Q3).
> 4. **Similar swap = BV + cash; dissimilar swap = FV + cash** (Q6 vs Q7).
> 5. **Revaluation surplus never touches P&L** — on disposal the whole surplus goes to General Reserve, with the sale gain/loss booked separately in P&L; the *net* into reserves = proceeds − original BV (Q5).
