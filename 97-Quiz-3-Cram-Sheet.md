# 97 · Quiz 3 — One-Page Cram Sheet

## Acquisition — what capitalises
**Test:** "needed to get it *ready to work* for the first time?" Yes → capitalise (invoice net of discount, freight, installation *wages*, site prep, testing). No → expense (overhead, own profit margin, opportunity cost, training, **fuel/running costs**, later repairs, **relocation of a working asset**). *Land:* + demolition of worthless building + permanent improvements; never depreciated.

## Disposal — remove at GROSS
`Dr Cash + Dr Accumulated Dep + (Dr Loss / Cr Gain) → Cr Asset at gross cost.` Gain/Loss = **Cash − NBV**. It's P&L but **not revenue**; never "extraordinary"; never net a gain against a loss.

## Exchange (trade-in)
- **Dissimilar** (commercial substance) → new asset = **FV of old + cash** (recognise gain/loss).
- **Similar** (no commercial substance) → new asset = **BV of old + cash** (no gain/loss).
- Trade-in allowance is only a proxy for FV when FV is unknown.

## Depreciation methods
| | Formula |
|---|---|
| SLM | (Cost − Residual) ÷ life — flat |
| WDV rate (from cost) | $1-(R/C)^{1/n}$, applied to **opening book value** |
| WDV rate (switch mid-life) | $1-(\text{residual}/\text{carrying amount})^{1/\text{remaining life}}$ |
| Production-unit | (Cost − Residual)/total output × output this year |
| Part-year | annual × months/12 (Indian FY ends 31 Mar) |
- WDV front-loads the charge; only **WDV allowed for tax** in India.

## Change in estimate OR method — PROSPECTIVE
Freeze the past → **(carrying amount − revised residual) ÷ remaining life**. Never restate; no entry on the change date. **Trap:** switching to WDV, use *carrying amount & remaining life*, not cost/original life.

## Revaluation
- **Up → Revaluation Reserve (OCI)**; **Down → P&L** (unless reversing this asset's own surplus → reduce reserve first).
- Whole **class**, policy choice. Depreciate on **revalued amount ÷ remaining life**.
- **Excess depreciation** (new − old-cost) transferred **Reserve → General Reserve** each year → distributable profit unchanged.
- **On disposal:** to General Reserve = **proceeds − original book value**.

## Impairment
- Impaired if **carrying amount > recoverable amount**.
- **Recoverable amount = HIGHER of** net selling price and **value in use** (PV of future cash flows at pre-tax rate).
- Loss hits **Revaluation Reserve first, then P&L**.
- **Goodwill/CGU:** test at CGU level; write down **goodwill first**, excess pro-rata to other assets; **never reversed**. (Other impairments: Ind AS may reverse; US GAAP never.)

## Intangibles
- **Acquired → capitalise; internally generated brand/goodwill → expense** (software excepted). Research **expense**, development **capitalise** (feasible + saleable).
- Amortise over **useful life or legal life, whichever shorter**. Trademark → useful life governs; franchise → contract term.
- **AS vs IFRS:** goodwill — AS amortise ≤5 yrs / IFRS impairment-only; other intangibles — AS ≤10 yrs / IFRS finite-amortise, indefinite-impair.

## Delta vs Singapore
Dep per $100 = **100(1−salvage%)/life**. 1993: Delta **$4.75**, Singapore **$8.00**. Longer life / higher salvage → less depreciation → **higher profit now**, but lower book value → **bigger gain on sale** later (Singapore's fast-fleet strategy).
