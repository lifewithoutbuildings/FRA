## The paper

### Q1. What goes into cost? (2 marks)
A firm buys a machine: invoice ₹4,80,000 (2% cash discount taken), freight ₹6,000, installation wages ₹9,000, installation overhead allocation ₹4,000, staff training ₹5,000, first month's electricity ₹3,000. **What is the capitalised cost?**

### Q2. Disposal (2 marks)
Equipment: gross cost ₹7,50,000, accumulated depreciation ₹5,10,000, sold for ₹1,80,000. **Journalise the disposal and state the gain/loss.**

### Q3. Change in method, SLM → WDV (3 marks)
Plant ₹6,00,000, residual ₹60,000, life 5 yrs, SLM. At the start of year 3 the firm switches to WDV, residual unchanged. **Compute the year-3 depreciation.**

### Q4. MCQ — capitalise or expense? (1 mark)
Which is **capital** expenditure?
a. Repainting the factory  b. Replacing a broken windowpane  c. Adding a mezzanine floor that raises capacity  d. Annual servicing

### Q5. Revaluation + depreciation (4 marks)
Building cost ₹10,00,000, life 20 yrs, residual nil, SLM. At the start of **year 5** it is revalued to ₹12,00,000 (residual still nil). **Compute: (a) carrying amount just before revaluation, (b) revaluation reserve created, (c) depreciation for year 5, (d) the annual reserve→general-reserve transfer.**

### Q6. Impairment (3 marks)
Machine carrying amount ₹40 lacs. Net selling price ₹28 lacs. Value in use ₹31 lacs. **(a) Recoverable amount? (b) Impairment loss? (c) If a revaluation reserve of ₹5 lacs exists for this machine, how is the loss split?**

### Q7. Exchange (2 marks)
A firm trades in an old **printer** (BV ₹80,000, FV ₹70,000) plus ₹1,50,000 cash for a new **printer** (invoice ₹2,40,000). **At what value is the new printer recorded, and why?**

### Q8. MCQ — intangibles (1 mark)
Which may be **capitalised**?
a. Internally generated goodwill  b. A purchased patent  c. Research costs  d. Cost of building an in-house brand

### Q9. MCQ — Delta/Singapore (1 mark)
An airline lengthens the depreciable life of its fleet and raises salvage %. The **immediate** effect on reported profit is:
a. lower  b. higher  c. unchanged  d. cannot say

### Q10. MCQ — impairment vs US GAAP (1 mark)
Under Ind AS, an impairment loss (not goodwill) that later reverses:
a. can never be reversed  b. may be reversed  c. is reversed only for land  d. reverses automatically

## Answer key (expand after attempting)

> [!success]- Q1 — **₹4,85,400**
> Invoice net of 2% = 4,80,000 × 0.98 = 4,70,400; + freight 6,000 + installation **wages** 9,000 = **₹4,85,400**. **Out:** overhead allocation (4,000), training (5,000), electricity (3,000) — general/running costs.

> [!success]- Q2 — **loss ₹60,000**
> NBV = 7,50,000 − 5,10,000 = 2,40,000; cash 1,80,000 → **loss 60,000**.
> ```
> Dr Cash 1,80,000 / Dr Accum.Dep 5,10,000 / Dr Loss 60,000 → Cr Equipment 7,50,000
> ```

> [!success]- Q3 — **₹1,77,174**
> SLM dep = (6,00,000 − 60,000)/5 = 1,08,000/yr → 2 yrs → accumulated 2,16,000 → carrying **3,84,000**, remaining life 3 yrs.
> WDV rate (from carrying amount, remaining life) = 1 − (60,000/3,84,000)^(1/3) = **0.4614 (46.14%)**.
> Year-3 dep = 3,84,000 × 0.4614 ≈ **₹1,77,174**. *(Using the cost-based rate 1−(60,000/6,00,000)^(1/5)=36.9% would be **wrong** — that's the whole trap.)*

> [!success]- Q4 — **c** (mezzanine raises capacity = capital). a, b, d are repairs/maintenance = revenue expenditure.

> [!success]- Q5 — (a) **₹8,00,000** (b) **₹4,00,000** (c) **₹75,000** (d) **₹25,000**
> Annual SLM dep = 10,00,000/20 = 50,000. After 4 yrs accumulated 2,00,000 → **carrying 8,00,000** (a).
> Revalue to 12,00,000 → **reserve = 12,00,000 − 8,00,000 = 4,00,000** (b).
> Remaining life 16 yrs → new dep = 12,00,000/16 = **75,000** (c).
> Excess depreciation = 75,000 − 50,000 = **25,000** transferred reserve→general reserve each year (d).

> [!success]- Q6 — (a) **₹31 lacs** (b) **₹9 lacs** (c) ₹5 lacs to reserve, **₹4 lacs to P&L**
> Recoverable = max(28, 31) = **31**; impairment = 40 − 31 = **9**. With a ₹5-lac reserve: reserve absorbs 5 (OCI), remaining **4 → P&L**.

> [!success]- Q7 — **₹2,30,000**
> Similar assets (printer for printer) → **no commercial substance** → **BV + cash** = 80,000 + 1,50,000 = **₹2,30,000**; no gain/loss; fair value and invoice ignored.

> [!success]- Q8 — **b** (purchased patent). Internal goodwill, research, in-house brand are all expensed.

> [!success]- Q9 — **b** (higher). Longer life + higher salvage → lower annual depreciation → higher reported profit now (the Delta move).

> [!success]- Q10 — **b** (may be reversed under Ind AS; goodwill never; US GAAP never).
