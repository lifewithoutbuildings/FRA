## What was covered (Day 4 takeaways)

- **Impairment:** a one-time reduction in an asset's value from obsolescence or major damage. Test = compare **book value** with **recoverable amount**; if recoverable < book value, it's impaired. **Recoverable amount = higher of fair disposal value and value in use.** Losses route through P&L — but a **Revaluation Reserve absorbs them first**. → [[17-Impairment-of-Assets]]
- **Accounting for intangible assets** — acquired are recognised, internally generated (brand, goodwill) are not; research expensed, development capitalised; amortise over useful life. → [[18-Intangible-Assets]]
- **A fun quiz** was run to revise the whole long-term-assets block. → [[95-Fun-Quiz-Session-14-Worked]]
- Reading: Narayanaswamy pp. 255–256 (impairment); "Intangible Assets: A Search for Right Accounting" (Bhattacharyya).
