Acquisition and Disposal through the case of Stafford Press

Acquisition: Basic principle is to capitalize all costs incurred to bring the asset to its productive use. 

➢ Disposal: Example: Suppose land value in the balance sheet is Rs 5,00,000 and it is sold for RS 7,00,000, then it should be treated as follows:

Cash                                          Dr 7,00,000

Land                                                                  Cr 5,00,000

Gain on sale of land                                        Cr 2,00,000

Another example:

Suppose gross equipment value as per balance sheet is Rs 2,00,000. Accumulated depreciation as per balance sheet is Rs 80,000. Then book value is Rs 1,20,000. Assume it is sold for Rs 90,000. Treatment:

Cash                                                             Dr 90,000

Accumulated Depreciation                      Dr 80,000

Loss on sale of equipment                       Dr 30,000

Equipment                                                                       Cr 2,00,000

Notice that, the asset value getting credit is the gross valueand not the net carrying value. This is because the entire asset needs to be removed from the books, as well as, the accumulated depreciation on the same needs to be removed. 

Additional points:

➢ Reinstallation, moving expenses, repairs are treated as expenses as these do not increase the productive capacity of the asset. 

➢ Change in assumption of residual value has an impact on depreciation amount.

Stafford Press continued and completed. 

➢ Exchange of assets were discussed. 

It is important to identify whether the exchange involves commercial substance.

The value assigned to the new asset for dissimilar asset is the fair value plus the cash paid. Trade-in allowance is not the information on fair value.

Note: Of course, if there is no information available on the fair value, the proxy that we can depend on is the trade-in allowance mentioned by the vendor. If information on fair value is ascertainable the trade-in allowance is not considered. 

For similar assets, the value assigned to the new asset is book value plus the cash paid. 

Meaning of depreciation was discussed.

Depreciation is the allocation of the cost of the asset over its useful life.
